Who We Are

A trademark is much more than a name or a logo: it represents the identity of a business, the value behind a brand, and one of the most important intellectual property assets of a company.

ESTUDIO POI is a law firm specializing in Intellectual Property and Trademark Law, providing strategic trademark services to entrepreneurs, professionals, startups, and companies seeking to protect and develop their brands in the United States and internationally.

We assist our clients throughout the trademark lifecycle — from trademark clearance and availability searches to filing and prosecution before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), registration, portfolio management, monitoring, and enforcement.

Our approach combines legal expertise with a practical understanding of our clients' business objectives. We provide personalized, strategic, and transparent advice, tailored to each client's needs and stage of growth.

We believe trademark protection should be more than simply filing an application. It requires a well-planned strategy designed to minimize risks, protect the value of your brand, and support its long-term growth.

At ESTUDIO POI, we don't just register trademarks — we build strategies to protect your brand and give you the legal security you need to focus on growing your business.

Core Services

Trademark Clearance Search

We assess the availability and registrability of your trademark before filing, identifying potential conflicts and reducing risks to improve the chances of securing registration.

Trademark Registration

We manage the entire U.S. trademark registration process before the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), from application filing through examination and registration.

Trademark Renewals

We monitor renewal deadlines and handle the required filings to help maintain your trademark registration and keep your protection in force.

Trademark Monitoring

We monitor newly filed trademark applications and registrations to identify potentially conflicting marks and help protect your rights.

Trademark Enforcement & Defense

We provide representation and strategic assistance in oppositions, cancellations, cease-and-desist matters, negotiations, and other trademark disputes, developing appropriate strategies to protect your brand against third parties.

International Trademark Protection

We advise businesses seeking to protect their trademarks in the United States and other international markets, developing filing and protection strategies tailored to their business objectives.
